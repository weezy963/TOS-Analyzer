# TOS-Analyzer
Analyzes terms of services or other legal documents

## Overview

The TOS Analyzer is a web application designed to help users understand the often complex and lengthy Terms of Service agreements.  It provides a simplified summary and highlights key concerns, making it easier to grasp the implications of these legal documents.

## Features

* **Terms of Service Analysis**: Analyzes Terms of Service documents.
* **Simplified Summaries**: Provides easy-to-understand summaries of the key points.
* **Privacy Concern Highlights**: Identifies potential privacy risks and concerns.
* **User-Friendly Interface**: Simple and intuitive design for ease of use.
* **Upload and Paste Support**: Users can either upload a document or paste the text.

## Technologies Used

* React.js
* Tailwind CSS

## Usage

1.  Upload a Terms of Service document or paste the text into the input area.
2.  Click the "Analyze Terms" button.
3.  View the summarized analysis and highlighted concerns.
